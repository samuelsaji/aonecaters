About A-one

A family kitchen that grew into a full catering team

Since 1999 we've cooked for weddings, feasts and family days across Kerala. The recipes have stayed the same. The team has grown.

Our story

“Let us be your personal chef for your special occasion”

That line has been our promise from the first wedding we cooked for. A host should spend the day with guests, not in the kitchen. So we take the whole food side off your hands: planning the menu, buying fresh, cooking, serving and cleaning up.

Over the years we've added reception buffets, live counters and event support, but our kitchen still runs on the dishes Kerala families know and love.

Our journey

Twenty-seven years of feasts

1999

A-one Caterers begins in Kothamangalam, cooking sadya and biryani for local weddings.

Growing

More families, more venues. We add buffet service, non-veg banquets and a trained serving team.

A-one Caterers & Events

Event support joins catering, so hosts can plan food and the day with one team.

Today

Events from 10 guests to more than 1,000 plates, in Kothamangalam and across Kerala.
