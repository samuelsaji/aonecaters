Our signature

The Kerala sadya, served on banana leaf

Every leaf is served in order, the way it should be: salt and banana chips first, curries in their place, two payasams to finish. Tell us your guest count and we'll plan the rows, the servers and the timing.

Our story

Cooking for Kerala's big days since 1999

A-one started as a family kitchen in Kothamangalam. Today we cater weddings, celebrations and corporate events wherever our clients need us, with the same recipes and the same care.

  • Traditional recipes, cooked the way your family expects
  • Veg, non-veg and mixed menus
